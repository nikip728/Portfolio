This self-initiated redesign focuses on improving visual clarity and usability in the Pinterest mobile app. As a regular Pinterest user, I wanted to explore how the app could better support inspiration and content discovery without overwhelming users. This conceptual project is part of my design portfolio, showcasing my full UX/UI process from research and personas to wireframes and final interface design.
